## Sources and rationale

- [KDIGO 2012 AKI guideline](https://kdigo.org/wp-content/uploads/2016/10/KDIGO-2012-AKI-Guideline-English.pdf), section 2.1 and Table 2 (printed p19): AKI timing; Stage 2 creatinine 2.0–2.9 times baseline. Stage 3 is 3 times baseline, creatinine at least 4 mg/dL, RRT, or qualifying severe urine-output criteria. Section 2.3: evaluate cause, monitor creatinine and urine output. Section 3.1.1: isotonic crystalloids for intravascular volume expansion; 3.4.2: do not use diuretics to treat AKI except for volume overload. Sections 5.1.1–5.1.2: emergency RRT for life-threatening fluid/electrolyte/acid-base disturbances; consider clinical context and trends, not a single BUN/creatinine threshold.
- [KDIGO current AKI page](https://kdigo.org/guidelines/acute-kidney-injury/) describes the update as a public-review draft. This correction uses the published 2012 criteria, NOT a draft as final guidance.
- [MOH Malaysia / Malaysian Society of Nephrology / Academy of Medicine Malaysia, Management of CKD, second edition (2018)](https://www.moh.gov.my/images/04-penerbitan/penerbitan-klinikal/panduan-amalan-klinikal/CPG%20Management%20of%20Chronic%20Kidney%20%20Disease%20Second%20Edition.pdf), section 4.7: medication review and NSAID avoidance; identifies volume depletion and concurrent NSAID/diuretic exposure as kidney-injury risks in the contrast context. This supports local medication-safety context, **not an AKI staging or BUN-ratio guideline**. Copyright MaHTAS; paraphrased here without endorsement. No dedicated current MOH adult AKI CPG was located in the inspected official CPG listing.
- [Manoeuvrier et al., BMC Nephrology 2017;18:173](https://doi.org/10.1186/s12882-017-0591-9): BUN/creatinine ratio did not reliably distinguish prerenal from intrinsic AKI in the studied ED population. Hence preserve the actual laboratory numbers and remove the false diagnostic certainty rather than inflate BUN to fit a mnemonic.
